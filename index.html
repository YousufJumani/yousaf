Who's writing this?

Author · Yousuf Nasurullah
Affiliation · NED University

I'm a software engineering student at NED University of Engineering & Technology, currently in my third year, and I've spent the last few years building things — some of them useful, all of them interesting. My work tends to sit at the edges: edge computing, edge cases, edge of what an intern is supposed to be trusted with.

I care about the texture of software. Not just whether it works, but whether it feels right. Whether the loading state is considered. Whether the copy is honest. Whether a stranger can find what they need in the first thirty seconds.

"Good software is honest software. It doesn't promise what it can't deliver, and it delivers what it promises — quietly, every time."

I've shipped things across AI, web, and automation — the kind of work that requires you to read three docs, argue with two APIs, and then explain it all to someone who just wants the button to work. That last part is the interesting part. That's where the engineering becomes human.
